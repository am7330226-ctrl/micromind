# 🧠 MicroMind Laptop Browser Extension (Manifest V3)

A **Chrome / Microsoft Edge Extension** that pins **MicroMind** right onto your laptop screen as a Side Panel or Toolbar Popup.

---

## ✨ Extension Features

- ⚡ **Screen Extension (Side Panel)**: Keep your Eisenhower matrix & tasks open on the right side of your laptop screen while working or browsing.
- 🎯 **Quick Toolbar Popup**: Click the 🧠 icon on your browser toolbar for fast task capture, micro-habits, and Pomodoro focus timer.
- 🖱️ **Right-Click Context Menu**: Select any text on any website -> Right-click -> **"🧠 Add to MicroMind Inbox"**.
- 🤖 **On-Device AI Classification**: Automatically categorizes captured tasks into Eisenhower quadrants.

---

## 🛠️ How to Load in Google Chrome / Microsoft Edge

1. Open your browser and navigate to:
   - **Chrome**: `chrome://extensions`
   - **Edge**: `edge://extensions`
2. Enable **Developer mode** (toggle switch in the top-right corner).
3. Click **Load unpacked**.
4. Select the directory:
   `c:\Users\PW\OneDrive\Desktop\micromind-2\MicroMindExtension`
5. Pin the **MicroMind** icon (🧠) to your browser toolbar!

---

## 💡 How to Use as a Screen Extension

1. **Toolbar Popup**: Click the 🧠 icon on your extension bar for instant task brain-dumping.
2. **Side Panel**: Right-click the extension icon -> select **"Open side panel"** (or click the Side Panel button inside the popup) to keep MicroMind open right beside your browser tabs on your laptop screen!
